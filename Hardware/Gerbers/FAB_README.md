# TP-CAN-2I - Fabrication Gerber Package (REAL COPPER)

Generated by `generate_pcb.py`. Unlike the earlier mechanical-only set, these
layers contain **real copper**: every component land pattern is flashed, the
four copper layers carry pours/planes and routing, solder mask and paste are
matched to the pads, and the drill files are populated.

## Board
| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 70.0 x 50.0 mm, R3 corners |
| Layers | 4 (Sig / GND / PWR / GND) |
| Thickness | 1.6 mm, FR-4 Tg>=150 |
| Copper | 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner |

## What is in this package
- **F_Cu**: 423 flashes - SMD + THT land patterns, via lands, and routed
  power tree, SPI2 bus, CAN TXD/RXD, isolated CAN_H/CAN_L pairs, USB pair, UART.
- **In1_Cu (GND)** and **B_Cu (GND)**: split into three galvanically isolated
  ground domains - LOGIC | CAN1-ISO | CAN2-ISO - with the pours pulled back
  >4 mm around the x=54 mm barrier and the two milled isolation slots.
- **In2_Cu (PWR)**: +3V3 / +5V logic rails plus the two isolated +5V_ISO pours.
  Antipad clearances are punched around every non-matching drilled hole; matching
  power/ground holes connect directly to their plane.
- **F_Mask / B_Mask / F_Paste**: openings/stencil for all pads (vias tented).
- **Edge_Cuts**: outline + the two isolation milling slots under U9/U10.
- **PTH.drl (117 holes)** and **NPTH.drl (4x M3 Ø3.2)**.

## Isolation (spec section 7)
The two CAN channels are separated from the logic domain and from each other.
The ISO1042B transceivers and A0505S isolated DCDC converters are the only parts
that bridge the barrier; no plane copper crosses the slot/barrier gap.

## IMPORTANT - review before production
This is a programmatically generated physical layout: land patterns, pours,
vias, mask, paste and drill are real and self-consistent. Pad-to-pin **net
assignment follows the engineering spec's pin map but MUST be cross-checked
against the schematic/netlist** (IPC-356) before committing to fabrication, and
a Gerber viewer (KiCad / Gerbv / the fab's online viewer) should be used to
confirm clearances and the isolation gap for your chosen creepage rating.

Components: 92 placements, 45 routed segments.
